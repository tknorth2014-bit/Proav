Overview

Built for Comfort, Engineered for Distance

The Dassault Falcon 2000EX EASy combines a wide, stand-up cabin with the range to fly nonstop from Florida to the West Coast, Canada, the Caribbean, and much of South America.

Its Honeywell-based EASy flight deck gives our pilots exceptional situational awareness, while twin Pratt & Whitney PW308C engines deliver smooth, efficient performance.
