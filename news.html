Five Reasons Business Travelers Fly Private

Time is the one resource you can’t get back. Here’s how private charter gives it back to you.

Read More →

Private aviation lets you depart on your schedule, from airports closer to your meetings, and skip the terminal entirely. Many trips that take two days on the airlines can be done in one.

The cabin also becomes a private workspace: you can hold confidential conversations, review documents, and arrive prepared rather than worn out.

Finally, charter offers flexibility. If a meeting runs long, the aircraft waits for you, not the other way around.

How to Choose the Right Aircraft for Your Trip

Midsize or large cabin? A quick guide to matching the aircraft to your mission.

Read More →

Start with three questions: how many passengers, how far, and how much baggage. A midsize jet like the Citation XLS is ideal for regional and domestic trips with up to eight passengers.

For longer trips or larger groups, a large-cabin jet like the Falcon 2000EX EASy offers nonstop transcontinental range and a stand-up cabin with full galley.

What to Expect on Your First Private Charter

From arrival to touchdown, here’s what flying private actually looks like.

Read More →

You’ll arrive at a private terminal (FBO) instead of the main airline terminal, typically just 15 to 20 minutes before departure. Bring a valid government ID.

Your crew will greet you, load your bags, and brief you on the flight. Catering, ground transportation, and special requests are arranged ahead of time.

When you land, your car can often meet you planeside so you’re on your way within minutes.
