Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance do I need to book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, but we can often arrange flights with just a few hours' notice depending on aircraft and crew availability.

How is charter pricing calculated?

Pricing is based primarily on flight time, plus applicable fees such as federal excise tax, landing and handling fees, and crew expenses. Your quote is all-inclusive with no surprises.

Do I need to go through airport security?

Private charter passengers depart from a private terminal (FBO). You'll need valid government ID, but you avoid the commercial terminal and TSA lines.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes. Pets are welcome on board. Just let us know when booking so we can prepare the cabin.

Do you fly internationally?

Yes. The Falcon 2000EX has the range for Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, and Central and South America. We help with customs and documentation.
